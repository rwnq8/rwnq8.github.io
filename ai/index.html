Summary

Rowan Brad Quni-Gudzinas is a research systems builder with 15 years in national data and policy research: research portfolio manager at the U.S. Federal Highway Administration (2011 to 2015) and product manager of the AARP Livability Index (2016 to 2021). Since 2024: founder of QNFO, an open research imprint whose AI-assisted pipeline drafts, referees, revises and corrects papers, with each claim's status stated. Current research question: what a correct computation really costs, measured as energy per correct answer (the JPCUB benchmark).
